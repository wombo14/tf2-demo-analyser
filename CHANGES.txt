DEMO ANALYSIS WITH 3D PLAYER - CHANGES
======================================

This is a modified version of Demo Analysis by Fidoo001 and
contributors (https://github.com/Fidoo001/tf2-demo-analyser), which is
licensed under the GNU GPL version 3. This file lists everything this
version changes compared with the original at commit 2c17d9e
(7 November 2026).

In short: it adds a built-in 3D demo player, so you can watch any
detection without opening TF2. No detection check was added, removed or
changed. Every check, and what it finds, is the original's.


WHAT'S DIFFERENT
----------------

Watching demos in 3D (new)
  - "Watch demo" in the main window plays the whole demo, with every
    detection marked on the timeline. The play button next to a
    detection jumps to 3 seconds before it, through that player's eyes.
  - The match is rebuilt from the demo file and TF2's installed game
    files: the real map with its textures, lighting, props and sky,
    TF2's class models and weapons, and rockets, stickies, buildings,
    doors, carts and pickups. TF2 has to be installed but never opens.
    Cosmetics and hats are left out on purpose.
  - Watch through any player's eyes, with their hands and weapon firing,
    reloading and switching like in TF2, TF2's crosshair, and the Sniper
    scope when they zoom in. Or watch them in third person, from behind,
    and drag to swing the camera around them like spectating. Or fly a
    free camera.
  - Animations come from TF2's own files: running, crouching, jumping,
    a pose for each weapon (picked from TF2's item list), aiming,
    shooting, melee swings, reloads and TF2's death animations. They
    blend into each other the way TF2's do.
  - Dead players fall as ragdolls, thrown by the killing blow, and land
    on the map. Sentries turn to aim and fire, and buildings play their
    build animations.
  - Kill feed, chat, and a player list with class, health and the
    scoreboard ping of every player at the moment you're watching.

Seeing what a check saw (new, in the Show... menu)
  - Bullet lines and projectile trails (T), bullet impacts and damage
    numbers.
  - Team glow through walls: everyone, enemies, teammates or off (G).
  - Aim lines from every player's eyes (A), TF2's real hitboxes (H),
    and backtrack ghosts, which show where the server puts enemies when
    it judges the watched player's shots.
  - Aim graph: how fast the watched player's view turns, with their
    shots, hits and detections marked. An aimbot snap shows as one tall
    spike.
  - Aimbot FOV circle: for a flagged player, a circle around the
    crosshair as big as the biggest flick in their aim detections.

Sound (new)
  - The 3D player plays the match's sound from TF2's own sound files:
    guns, rockets, explosions, footsteps, voice lines, sentries, pickups
    and doors, quieter with distance and from the side they happen on.
    Voice chat from the demo plays too, with who's talking shown on
    screen. Volume and mute (M) are in the top bar; hit and kill sounds
    and voice chat can be turned off in the Show... menu.

Review mode (new)
  - Pick a player and the 3D player plays each of their detections in
    turn: slow motion from their eyes, a pause on the flagged moment,
    then a little after. A bar says which check fired, what to look for
    on screen, and what the check measured in plain words (angles,
    distances, ticks, scores and the target's name).

Reporting (new)
  - Right-click a player in the 3D player to export an HTML report
    (detections, kills, damage, accuracy and a ping chart), mark them
    in your TF2BD lists, open their Steam profile or copy their
    SteamID64.
  - Save clip... saves 5, 10 or 20 seconds around the current moment as
    an MP4 (needs ffmpeg) or a GIF.

Main window
  - The TF2BD Community Biglist and TF2BD Trusted lists are the default
    TF2BD playerlists. Configure playerlists has buttons to add them
    back if you remove them.
  - It tells you when Fidoo001's project has new changes ("N upstream
    updates" next to Open...), and updates itself: "Update available"
    downloads the newest release, checks its checksum and restarts,
    keeping the previous version as "Demo Analysis (previous).exe".
  - A robot on the GitHub repository checks Fidoo001's project every
    day, merges his changes, runs the tests and opens a pull request;
    merging it builds and publishes a new release.
  - Fixed: the skiesred/oob_pitch check had no tooltip, which made one
    of the original's own tests fail. It now has one.


HISTORY (newest first)
----------------------

9 November 2026.
  - Added review mode.
  - Added aim lines, hitboxes, backtrack ghosts, the aim graph, damage
    numbers, bullet impacts and the Show... menu.
  - Added Save clip..., player reports, marking in TF2BD from the 3D
    player, and the update check.
  - Added shooting, melee and reload animations. Jumps now start exactly
    when the jump key was pressed.
  - Fixed: the Heavy's minigun in first person now sits low on the
    right with the barrel forward. This is an approximation, because
    TF2's own first-person animation stands it on end.
  - Fixed: the arrow keys in the key hints showed as boxes.
  - Fixed animations: a reload or swing no longer carries over after a
    weapon switch, or plays on a weapon that has none (like a shotgun
    reload on a carried building). A scoped Sniper uses TF2's scoped
    fire and reload. Alternate attacks (the Pyro's airblast, the Spy's
    backstab, the Heavy's other fist, the Sandman's ball) use their own
    animations. Normal jumps no longer look like blast jumps, and a
    player with no class yet is no longer drawn as a pillar.
  - Movement animations now blend into each other over 0.2 seconds
    instead of snapping when someone stops, lands or crouches.
  - Dead bodies are ragdolls instead of all playing the same frozen
    animation. Explosions throw them; bullets and melee knock them
    back. Headshots, backstabs and burning deaths keep TF2's own
    death animations.
  - Sentries turn and tilt to aim where the server says they aimed,
    sentries, dispensers and teleporters play their build animations,
    and teleporters spin when ready.
  - Fixed: bullet lines, impacts, aim lines and bodies passed through
    terrain (hills, rocky ground) and solid brush objects like bridges.
  - First person: everyone's hands now draw, fire, swing and reload with
    TF2's own first-person animations, a revved minigun spins, and the
    weapon trails behind quick turns and tucks in when looking up or
    down, as in TF2. Before, only the recording player's hands moved.
  - Sentries play their firing animation, with a muzzle flash and a
    bullet line to what they're shooting at.
  - Fixed bullet lines: they started at the shooter's eyes, so in third
    person they came out of their head. They now start at the gun's
    muzzle, as in TF2, and a bullet that hit a player stops at them
    instead of carrying on through to the wall behind.
  - Added an aimbot FOV estimate for flagged players: the biggest flick
    onto a target among their aim detections, which their aimbot
    reaches at least. It shows as a circle around the crosshair when
    watching them (Show... > Aimbot FOV circle), in review mode and in
    player reports.
  - Added a user guide, GUIDE.md, with screenshots of the main window,
    the 3D player, its menus, review mode, reports and clips.
  - Fixed projectiles: the Righteous Bison's and the Pomson's shots were
    drawn in the wrong place, and the Sandman's ball and the Wrap
    Assassin's ornament weren't drawn at all. Energy shots (Bison, Pomson,
    Cow Mangler, Short Circuit) now glow in their team's colour and the
    Dragon's Fury's fireballs glow orange, as in TF2, with trails to match.
    Trails also stop short of the camera, so a shot the watched player
    just fired no longer draws a line across the whole screen.
  - Fixed: demos on some community maps wouldn't open ("damaged"),
    because of a map model whose animations are kept in a separate file.
  - Added updates: the app offers each new release from
    github.com/wombo14/tf2-demo-analyser and installs it on a click. A
    daily robot brings in Fidoo001's changes as pull requests, and a
    release robot builds and publishes every change to main.
  - Added third person: watch a player from behind (First person / Third
    person next to View, or C), drag to swing the camera around them like
    spectating, scroll to zoom, double-click to go back behind them.
  - Added sound: the demo's own sounds (what the recording player's game
    heard), explosions, the recording player's own shots and swings,
    TF2's hit and kill sounds for the watched player, and voice chat.
  - The timeline follows the player you watch: a bar along its bottom
    shows when the demo has them (green alive, grey dead, empty while
    they're out of the recorder's view), and it shows only their kills and
    deaths ("Only their kills and deaths" in the bottom bar). Hovering a
    kill on the timeline names who killed whom with what.

8 November 2026
  - Updated to Fidoo001's latest code (commit 2c17d9e), which brought
    their machine-learning checks, their fixes and the GPL-3.0 license.
  - Added the ping column to the 3D player's player list.
  - Turned the lmao/ checks off by default.

7 November 2026
  - Added the built-in 3D demo player.
  - Fixed black and upside-down views, missing floors, the map's
    backdrop drawn too small, hidden map pieces being drawn, some
    textures not loading, and feet sliding while walking.
  - Added TF2's death animations, map objects the demo never sent, and
    the Sniper scope view.
  - Added hands, weapons and the crosshair in first person. Worn items
    such as the Pyro's Thermal Thruster no longer cover the view.
  - Added bullet lines and projectile trails, with on/off switches.
  - Fixed terrain lighting and added the maps' baked lighting on props.
    Fixed Spy and Demoman weapon poses, jumps and aiming stances.
  - Added team glow through walls.
  - Added the TF2BD community lists as defaults.
  - Added the lmao/ checks, ported from titanium_machine's
    Cheater_Detection (removed again on 9 November).
  - Made the first shareable zip.


FILES
-----

New
  src/player/                the 3D player:
    view.rs                  the player window, controls, overlays
                             and review mode
    recording.rs             reads the demo into a timeline
    map.rs                   loads the map, its lighting and props
    renderer.rs              draws the map, models, glow and traces
    skeleton.rs              skeletons, animations and hitboxes
    characters.rs            class models and weapons
    assets.rs                reads TF2's game files
    items.rs                 weapon poses from TF2's item list
    report.rs                player reports
    clip.rs                  saving clips
    review.rs                review mode's grouping and plain words
    ragdoll.rs               dead bodies falling as ragdolls
    script.rs                scripted clicks and keys that take the
                             guide's screenshots (DEMO_ANALYSIS_SCRIPT)
    audio.rs                 playing sounds: decoding and mixing
    demo_sounds.rs           reading the sounds a demo carries
    mod.rs                   lists the files above
  src/bin/player_preview.rs  a developer tool that saves screenshots
                             and photographs every pose in a demo
  GUIDE.md                   the user guide
  docs/images/               the guide's screenshots
  packaging/                 the download zip's README and packing script
  .github/workflows/         the daily check of Fidoo001's project
                             (upstream.yml) and the release builder
                             (release.yml)

Changed
  Cargo.toml                 the 3D player's libraries (including cpal
                             for sound output and symphonia for MP3),
                             sha2 for checking updates, and the
                             "player" feature
  Cargo.lock                 the exact versions of those libraries
  src/lib.rs                 adds the 3D player, and builds voice chat
                             decoding (src/util/voice.rs) for it too,
                             not only for the experimental build
  src/bin/gui.rs             Watch demo buttons, opening the player,
                             marking in TF2BD from the player, the
                             update check and updater, and scripted
                             input for the guide's screenshots
  src/tf2bd.rs               the community lists as defaults
  src/tf2bd_view.rs          buttons to add the community lists
  src/lib/algorithm.rs       the missing skiesred/oob_pitch tooltip

Everything else, including every check in src/algorithms/, is the
original's.
